Four Learns: About AI ('Ilm & Tabayyun) · To use AI (Amanah) · With AI (Ihsan) · Beyond AI (Adab & Akhlaq), which MOE calls the most important.

Assessment: ✕ No AI (national exams, proctored tests, all P1 to 3 work) · ✎ AI-assisted, declared (secondary coursework where allowed) · ✓ AI-integrated (practice, drafting and feedback from P4).

Always: think first, then AI · no personal data in external tools · tools vetted before use · ARK follows the students' level tier.
